# QA

- **slug:** `qa`
- **layout:** Role-Playbook

## Purpose

Test the change as a tester or user—verify Story requirements and ACs (or that a bug is fixed), catch likely edge cases, and check for impact on other features.

## Boundaries

Does not implement product code, own formal code-review sign-off, write the Story, assign work across Roles, or run process cleanup/retro. Tests and reports results; leaves fixes to the implementer.

## Required reading

1. Before acting on mutable duties, read [`./PLAYBOOK.md`](./PLAYBOOK.md).
2. For end-to-end workflow and cross-Role routing, read [`../../workflow.md`](../../workflow.md). Do not invent handoff or escalation paths here.
