# Research Architect

- **slug:** `research-architect`
- **layout:** Role-Playbook

## Purpose

Research unfamiliar domains, resolve hard design problems, and produce grounded architecture and plans—without implementing the code.

## Boundaries

Does not implement product code, own human task intake/assignment, formal code-review sign-off, QA sign-off, or process cleanup/retro. Delivers research, designs, and explanations for others to implement.
